## Tiffany Koyama Lane

**Status:** Qualified incumbent, filed July 2, 2026.  
**Bio:** Koyama Lane is one of the three current District 3 councilors, elected in November 2024 and seated in January 2025. Her official City biography describes her as an award-winning public-school educator with **16 years of teaching experience**, a Portland Association of Teachers union leader, and a community organizer. The City also identifies her as the **first Asian American woman elected to Portland city government**. She served as the first Council Vice President under the new form of government and currently serves as vice chair of the Finance and Governance Committee of the Whole.  
**2024 history:** Elected to one of the three D3 seats in 2024.  
**SDE:** Certified; the City reports **$183,602** in matching funds distributed as of this snapshot.
