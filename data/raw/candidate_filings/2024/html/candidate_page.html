Visiting the Auditor’s Office at City Hall

We're open Monday to Friday, 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. We accept in-person visits Tuesday to Thursday at City Hall in room 130. For more information about how to connect with us either in person or remotely, please see our contact information on our homepage or visit our contact information webpage.

City candidate filing opens June 3, 2026

Candidate filing for City Auditor and City Councilors for Districts 3 and 4 opens 8:00 a.m. June 3, 2026. For more information, visit Run for City office. 

  • Filing closes for incumbents 5:00 p.m. on August 18.
  • Filing closes for non-incumbents 5:00 p.m. on August 25.

Official City of Portland Candidates and Measures Certified for the November 5, 2024 General Election 

On September 5, 2024, the City Elections Office certified City candidates and measures for the November 5, 2024 General Election ballot by filing the City Election Officer's Statement of Candidates, Positions, and Measures with the Multnomah County Elections Division.
