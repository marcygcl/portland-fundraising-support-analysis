Residence address exemption

Candidates must disclose their residence address as part of their declaration of candidacy.
Candidate declarations are publicly disclosed online. Candidates have the option to request their
residence address be exempt from disclosure by filing a separate Residence Address Exemption
Request with the Oregon Secretary of State. The exemption request must include a publicly
disclosable mailing address. Even if exemption requests are granted, residence addresses are still
subject to in-person inspection as a public record under ORS 192.311 to 192.478. An Address
Exemption Request must be filed within five (5) business days of filing this Candidate Filing
Application (AUD 120 Form) to avoid potential public posting of a residence address.
