Residence Address Exemption (Optional)

Candidates are required to disclose their residential address to verify qualification for candidacy. To
exempt your residence address from public disclosure, candidates must file a separate Residence
Address Exemption Request (SEL 180 Form) with the Secretary of State. The request for a Residence
Address Exemption must include a publicly disclosable mailing address. Residence addresses are still
subject to inspection as public record under ORS 192.311 to 192.478.
